Moreno Spirit: 1-hour boat trip

The walkways plus one hour on the water in front of the Perito Moreno wall, in a single day.

Destination
El Calafate
Duration
Full day (1 h on the water)
Difficulty
Easy

Along Route 11 we cross the steppe beside Lake Argentino, with a guide sharing the region's history and geology. First stop: Mirador de los Suspiros, overlooking Brazo Rico and the glacier's south face.

Then the National Park walkways (about 5 km on several levels) and a one-hour boat trip close to the wall of the Perito Moreno, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Highlights

  • Mirador de los Suspiros viewpoint
  • 1 hour on the water facing the glacier
  • National Park walkways
  • Wheelchair accessible (with a companion)
